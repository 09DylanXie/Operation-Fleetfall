# CLAUDE.md — Operation: Fleetfall

Read and follow all architectural rules, game design invariants, and GDScript coding conventions in [AGENTS.md](./AGENTS.md).

## Quick Reference

- **Engine:** Godot 4.7.2 (2D, GDScript with strict static typing)
- **Concept:** 2D WWII Naval RTS combining *Empire at War* macro territory control with *World of Warships* micro positioning, turret/broadside angles, and line-of-sight island cover.
- **Key Mechanics to Respect:**
  - **30-ship fleet cap** per player, **+3 per owned Major island** (any shipyard tier; `Match.fleet_cap()`, `GameBalance.major_island_fleet_bonus`); ships under construction are defenseless. **Class limits** (`ShipStats.max_active` / `max_per_game`, `Match.class_limit_reason()`): at most 2 SCV and 3 SBB at a time (afloat + queued), and 4 SCV launched per side per game (`Match.ships_launched`, synced as `built`).
  - **12 classes:** DD, SS, CL, CA, BC, BB, SBB, CV (Supercarrier, code `SCV`), RS, RD, LCV (Light Carrier), CLAA (AA Cruiser) (enum appends `BC`, `SBB`, `RS`, `RD`, `LCV`, `CLAA` after `CV`). The shop row and hotkeys `1`-`9`, `0`, `-`, `=` (`PlayerController.BUILD_KEYS`) follow `GameData.all_classes()`, smallest to largest: RD, RS, DD, SS, CL, CLAA, CA, BC, BB, SBB, LCV, SCV. `RS` Resource Ship and `RD` Radar Ship are unarmed support ships (`ShipStats.is_support()`): an RS stopped by a non-base island mines a Minor island's income, **one RS per island per side** (`Ship._update_mining_claim`: the first to anchor keeps it; other sides may share it), never on its own side's Minor/Major islands (`_in_mining_reach`); an RD spots surface ships to `1080`. Build times were halved; speeds CA `0.6375`, BC `0.513`, BB `0.4275`, SBB `0.38475`.
  - **Display:** 1920x1080 base canvas (scales to any window; opens at 1600x900), `CameraRig.DEFAULT_ZOOM` 2.016 (limits 0.72-4.6), art baked at 2x: world units, ranges and speeds are unchanged. The shop panel is one flat translucent row of build cards (`FlatButton` styles in `UITheme`).
  - **Player attack orders turn gun ships broadside** (`order_attack(target, true)` from the `attack` command; AI orders don't). Sub torpedoes fired at BB/SBB home `6s` at `120` deg/s (others: `2s`, `60`). `SBB` = BB with -10% speed, +30% shell damage, +3s reload, 30% armor, +30% cost, +10% HP and a flak barrage (2/5 kill chance per squadron every 10s). `BC` = 20% armor, +20% speed, BB reload, 4x quad turrets, -60% shell damage, -20% HP. Destroyers have 4 single guns.
  - **Visuals and sound are cosmetic only** (never touch `Match.rng` or game state): `ShipArt` vector art baked to sprites by `ShipArtCache`, turret training, fires, `ShipWreck` sinking, `IslandTerrain`, ocean shader, storms, the `Sfx` autoload (sounds synthesised in code, silent headless) and the `UITheme` look (keep the HUD fields tests read: `_info_label`, `_gold_label`, `_fleet_label`, `last_notice()`). The **swell** (`Match.wave_drift`) is simulation and deterministic.
  - **15-second economy tick** granting Gold and Steel based on captured Minor (Shallow Water) and Major (Deep Water) islands.
  - **Islands block both vision and projectiles** (hard cover).
  - **Focus-Fire Armor Stripping:** `-5%` armor per additional attacker beyond the first.
  - **Class-specific weapons:** Broadside auto-torpedoes on Destroyers (`30s` cooldown, half torpedo speed), bow/stern manual homing-ping torpedoes on Submarines (3 per launch, `2s` homing + `15s` reveal penalty, cannot attack Home Base), Destroyer depth charges that automatically hit spotted Submarines within `180` range, and Carrier air wings (Supercarrier `2028` range, launches every `3.33s` / `6.67s` / `2s`, bombs `165`, up to `5` dive squadrons stacked per target and several targets at once, `3` torpedo flights per launch over up to `3` targets with at most `3` per target; Light `1014` range, every `5s` / `10s` / `3s`, one torpedo flight, `5` squadrons total) that launch **automatically** at the nearest spotted enemy (a right-click overrides the target; a sunk target's squadrons retarget at once within reach): HE dive bombers (one every `5s`) hover over the target bombing every `3s` with armor-bypassing HE bombs (`150` damage); torpedo bombers (every `10s`, BC/BB/SBB/CV targets only) drop 2 parallel torpedoes off the broadside and fly home; depth-charge bombers (every `3s`) hit spotted subs. Planes fly over islands and can be shot down by CL/CA/BB anti-air (1 in 5 per attack run).
  - **AA Cruiser (`CLAA`):** a CL (same hull, HP, speed, armor, spotting, Tier 2, 15s) with no guns, costing a CA (`400G/250S`). Its flak barrage (`periodic_aa_*`, like the SBB's) downs each enemy squadron within `310` (half a CL's gun range) with a 1/3 chance every `3s`. It is not a support ship (`is_support()` excludes flak hulls); the AI builds one escort past 10 ships plus one per enemy carrier seen (max 3) and sails it in the middle of its attack wave.
  - **Order feedback (cosmetic):** with ships selected the cursor is a downward arrow, or a crosshair over a spotted enemy (`PlayerController.wanted_cursor`); move / dock / rally orders drop a small impact ripple and an attack order briefly brackets the target (`OrderFeedback`, `Match.order_feedback`, drawn above the fog). Shipyard docks look different per tier (`DockArt`: timber jetty, concrete wharf with crane, naval base with dry-dock gate and two hammerhead cranes).
  - **Island defenses:** a Major island with a Tier 3 shipyard is an air base (up to `3` dive-bomber squadrons, one per `10s`, at ships firing on it within `700`) and can buy radar (`200G/150S`, spotting `720`); both are lost with the island.
  - **Battleships, Battlecruisers and Super Battleships cannot target Submarines.** Battleships also carry `4` secondary guns (DD calibre, `8s` reload, range `470` = half their main guns) that fire on their own target.
  - **Spotting:** `DD 720`, `CL`/`CA 360`, `BB 240`, `CV 80`, `SS 0` - Destroyers scout for everything else.
  - **Docks:** a ship in a friendly dock repairs `2%` HP/s only if that shipyard's tier can build its class and the yard is not building.
  - **Start menu** (`scenes/menu.tscn`) picks the Small / Medium / Large map preset and 1-3 AI opponents; `scenes/main.tscn` is the match.
  - **2-4 sides on a round sea** with bases evenly on the rim; sides with the same `Match.alliance` id are allies - use `are_enemies()` / `is_enemy_of()`, never `team != other`.
  - **LAN multiplayer** (`Net` autoload, `scenes/lobby.tscn`): host-authoritative; clients run a `replica` Match fed by `NetSync` snapshots and send every action as a `Match.command()` Dictionary, validated by `execute_command`. New game state must be added to the snapshot.
  - **Fortified islands:** an owned island has a garrison (`2000` HP Minor / `4000` Major) that must be shot to 0 (subs cannot hurt it); the island then turns neutral, losing its shipyard, and only a neutral island can be captured.
  - **Batch 4:** single-player pause (`P`, `HUD.toggle_pause`, `SceneTree.paused`); Resource Ship mining marker; Supercarrier costs `1500G/600S`; Radar Ships see over islands (`sees_over_islands`); garrisons on Tier 2+ shipyard islands regenerate `5%`/s after `5s` unhit; the menu opens on a title screen with a Start button.
  - **AI difficulty:** Hard = original AI; Easy and Medium give an order to only one ship at a time (Medium 1 per 2s, Easy 1 per 5s; both plan production less often). Menu picks it; `--difficulty=easy|medium|hard`.
  - **30-second island capture** (neutral islands only); build orders are **paid in full when queued** (refunded in full on cancel, including the hull on the slip) and the next order starts the moment the previous ship launches.

Refer to [AGENTS.md](./AGENTS.md) for the complete Fleet Roster table, resource rates, and Godot 4 collision/architecture guidelines.
